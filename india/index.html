    About this piece

    A curtain for every Indian state — each one hung beneath one of its own well-known temples, woven from the script of the language actually spoken there.

    Move your cursor through the letters and they part like a beaded doorway curtain, then settle back and ring softly as they go.

    Design concept inspired by marinabudarina.github.io/chimes. Text, artwork, and code in this recreation are original.
